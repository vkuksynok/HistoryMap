Atlas of World History

See the events and historical figures that were happening at the same time, anywhere on Earth, from 3200 BC to today.

  • Map — drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom (levels 0–7), double-click to zoom in.
  • Timeline (bottom) — the golden window is the visible period:
    • drag the window to move through time,
    • drag its edges (or the − span / ＋ span buttons) to change the span,
    • click the ruler to jump there — scroll over the timeline to zoom its scale (drag pans when zoomed),
    • click a colored bar in the ribbon to open that event,
    • click an era name above the ribbon, or use the Period menu and the from–to fields, to select a specific period.
  • Markers — circles are events, rounded squares are historical figures. A pulsing ring means it began inside the current window. Click for details.
  • Play — watch history unfold year by year (Space).
  • Keyboard — ← / → nudge time, Shift+← / → jump a full window, +/− zoom.

All imagery and data are served locally — the app works fully offline.
